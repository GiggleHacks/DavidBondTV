Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for David Bond TV memberships. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

Cancelling your membership

  • You can cancel at any time from your account via the billing portal — no need to contact us.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. You keep full access to exclusive content until the end of your current paid period.
  • We do not automatically pro-rate or refund the unused part of a billing period when you cancel.

Refunds

Memberships provide immediate access to digital content. As a result, payments are generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable consumer-protection law.

We may, at our discretion, issue a refund in cases such as:

  • a duplicate or clearly erroneous charge;
  • a technical fault on our side that prevented you from accessing the content you paid for and that we could not resolve; or
  • where a refund is required by the law of your jurisdiction.

Statutory rights (EU/UK and others)

If you are a consumer in a region with a statutory withdrawal/"cooling-off" right, those rights still apply. Note that by starting to stream content immediately you may consent to begin the service before any cooling-off period ends, which can affect the right to withdraw for digital content already supplied.

How to request a refund

Email support@davidbondtv.com from the address on your account, with the date and amount of the charge. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first — we can usually resolve it faster than a bank dispute.
